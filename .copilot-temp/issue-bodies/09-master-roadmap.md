## 2026 product enhancement roadmap for Hi.EventsCM

This issue tracks the highest-value gaps identified from a review of the current Hi.Events codebase and a benchmark against Ticket Tailor.

## Recommended order of execution
1. Recurring events and timed-entry support
2. Reserved seating and seating chart MVP
3. Memberships, season passes, and gift cards
4. Box office / POS mode for in-person sales
5. Buyer self-service improvements
6. Wallet / mobile pass support
7. GA4, TikTok, and CRM integrations
8. Conference and venue operations pack

## Strategic note
The biggest near-term opportunity is to move Hi.Events from a strong open-source general-admission platform into a more venue-grade and repeat-revenue-capable product.
